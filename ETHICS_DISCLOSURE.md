## Plan

1. Send the short letter in `internal/EMAIL_TO_DATASET_AUTHORS.md` (version 1)
   to the corresponding author of the 5G-NIDD paper, from a human account.
   The letter contains the finding in five sentences and a ten-minute
   reproduction procedure that does not require redistributing any data.
2. Allow **three weeks** for a reply; send one short reminder after ten days.
3. Record the date here and in the paper. If there is no reply, submit with the
   claim stated as our own inference and note the contact attempt and its date.
4. If the authors confirm, cite their statement. If they dispute it, include
   their position in the paper and re-examine the evidence before submitting.

## Why this matters for this paper

The claim "these labels are wrong" rests on our own inference from three
independent observations, not on the dataset authors' confirmation. Giving them
a right of reply before publication is both fair and, for reviewers, the
difference between an inference and a verified finding.
